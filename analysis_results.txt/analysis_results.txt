Key Observations
1. Customers with shorter tenure showed higher churn in this sample.
2. Churned customers had higher average monthly charges than retained customers.
3. Month-to-month contracts showed more churn than longer-term contracts.
4. Electronic check and mailed check customers were present among churned customers.
5. Fiber optic customers represented a higher share of churned customers in this sample.

Business Insights
- Customer retention efforts can focus on newer customers.
- Pricing and service value should be reviewed for high-charge customers.
- Longer-term contracts may support customer retention.
- Customer segments with higher churn should be monitored regularly.
